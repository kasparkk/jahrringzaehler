Detection settings

More sensitivity finds faint rings but also picks up noise. Raise the min. spacing if one ring is counted twice. A wider strip averages out cracks and dirt.

Tips for a good count
  • Clean or sand the cut surface. Wetting it with water makes rings much clearer.
  • Photograph straight from above, without shadows, and fill the frame.
  • Pick a radius without cracks, knots or rot. Rings are often widest on one side, which is fine.
  • Zoom in near the bark: the outer rings are the narrowest and easiest to miss.
  • Check a second radius and compare. Real rings show up on both.
